Honey Glazed Chicken Thighs

Description

Juicy chicken thighs and drumsticks pan-fried to a golden crisp, then roasted with aromatic oregano, sweet red onions, and bright orange citrus. A drizzle of runny honey creates a sticky glaze that balances perfectly with the tangy squeeze of fresh orange juice. Finished with quartered cherry tomatoes, crunchy pistachios, and crumbled feta cheese, this dish offers a delightful mix of sweet, savory, and tangy flavors in every bite.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg mixed chicken thighs & drumsticks, skin on, bone in.
  • two red onions.
  • one bunch of oregano (20g).
  • two oranges.
  • 160 g ripe mixed-color cherry tomatoes.
  • two tablespoons of runny honey.
  • 20 g shelled unsalted pistachios.
  • 20 g feta cheese.

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Place the chicken in a large shallow casserole pan and set it on high heat.
  3. Fry the chicken for 10 minutes, ensuring to turn it regularly until it achieves a golden color all over.
  4. During the frying process, peel and roughly chop the onions, and pick the oregano leaves.
  5. Utilize a speed-peeler to strip the peel from the orange directly into the pan.
  6. Add the chopped onions, oregano leaves, and two tablespoons of red wine vinegar to the pan.
  7. Season the contents with salt and black pepper, then mix well.
  8. Ensure the chicken is positioned skin side up in the pan.
  9. Transfer the pan to the preheated oven and roast for 45 minutes, or until the chicken meat easily pulls away from the bone.
  10. After roasting, move the pan to a medium-high heat on the hob.
  11. Squeeze the orange juice into the pan.
  12. Quarter the tomatoes and add them to the pan, allowing the mixture to sizzle and bubble.
  13. Drizzle honey over the chicken.
  14. Finely chop the pistachios and scatter them over the chicken.
  15. Crumble feta cheese over the chicken.
